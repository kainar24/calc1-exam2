5 past problems, all of the same form: “find the average value of $f$ on $[a,b]$”, which in practice is a u-substitution (Fall 2025 Test 3 #7, Review #5, Fall 2022, Fall 2024).

What you need to know

  • $f_{\text{ave}}=\dfrac1{b-a}\displaystyle\int_a^bf(x)\,dx$. Don't forget the factor $\frac1{b-a}$.
  • MVT for integrals: if $f$ is continuous on $[a,b]$, there is $c$ with $f(c)=f_{\text{ave}}$.
  • Average velocity $=\frac1{t_1-t_0}\int v\,dt$ = (change in position)/(time).
Mean-Value Theorem for Integrals (5.8.2)
If $f$ is continuous on $[a,b]$, there is at least one $c\in[a,b]$ with $\int_a^bf(x)\,dx=f(c)(b-a)$.

Worked examples

Example 1 (Fall 2025 Test 3 #7, 12 pts): $f=x^2\sqrt{3x^3+1}$ on $[0,2]$
  1. $f_{\text{ave}}=\frac12\int_0^2x^2\sqrt{3x^3+1}\,dx$.
  2. $u=3x^3+1$, $du=9x^2dx$, $u:1\to25$.
  3. $\int=\frac19\cdot\frac23[u^{3/2}]_1^{25}=\frac2{27}(125-1)=\frac{248}{27}$.
  4. $f_{\text{ave}}=\frac{124}{27}$.

Marking scheme: setup 2, substitution 3, evaluation 4, final answer 3.

Example 2 (Review Ch5 #5): $f=\frac{x^2}{\sqrt{x^3+1}}$ on $[0,2]$

$\frac12\cdot\frac13\int_1^9u^{-1/2}du=\frac16[2\sqrt u]_1^9=\frac23$.

Example 3 (Fall 2022 Exam 3): average velocity, $v=e^{2t}+\sin t$ on $[0,\pi]$

$\frac1\pi\left[\frac12e^{2t}-\cos t\right]_0^\pi=\frac{e^{2\pi}+3}{2\pi}$.

Common mistakes
  • Forgetting $\frac1{b-a}$ (computing just the integral).
  • Not changing the limits in the substitution.
Exam tips
  • Write the formula with numbers first: “$f_{\text{ave}}=\frac1{2-0}\int_0^2\dots$”. That line alone is worth 2 points.
