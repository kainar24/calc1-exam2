MATH 161 · Exam 2 cheat sheet — Ch. 4 (no 4.3, 4.7) · Ch. 5 · §7.2–7.3 (Anton 10e)

4.1 Increase / concavity

$f'>0\Rightarrow f\nearrow$, $f'<0\Rightarrow f\searrow$ (on intervals).
$f''>0$ concave up ($f'\nearrow$), $f''<0$ concave down.
Inflection point: $f$ continuous and concavity changes (sign of $f''$ changes; $f''=0$ alone is not enough).
Graph of $f'$: $f\nearrow$ where $f'$ is above the axis; $f$ is CU where $f'$ is rising.

4.2 Relative extrema

Critical point: $f'(x_0)=0$ or $f'(x_0)$ does not exist ($x_0\in$ domain).
1st derivative test: $+\to-$ max, $-\to+$ min, no change: neither.
2nd derivative test ($f'(x_0)=0$): $f''>0$ min, $f''<0$ max, $f''=0$ inconclusive.

4.4 Absolute extrema

EVT: $f$ continuous on closed $[a,b]$ ⇒ abs max and min exist.
Method: critical points in $(a,b)$ + endpoints → table of values → largest / smallest.
Open/infinite interval: check limits at the ends; one relative extremum only ⇒ it is absolute.

4.5 Optimization

Picture → objective → constraint → one variable → domain → $f'=0$ → justify (closed interval: compare values; else $f''$ sign + end behaviour) → answer what was asked.
Open box $\ell\times w\times h$: $V=\ell wh$, area $=\ell w+2\ell h+2wh$. Cylinder: $V=\pi r^2h$, $S=2\pi r^2+2\pi rh$. Distance: minimize $D^2$.

4.6 Rectilinear motion

$v=s'$, $a=v'=s''$, speed $=|v|$. $v=0$: stopped / may turn around.
Speeding up: $v,a$ same sign; slowing down: opposite signs.

4.8 Rolle & MVT

Hypotheses: continuous on $[a,b]$, differentiable on $(a,b)$.
Rolle: $+\,f(a)=f(b)$ ⇒ $\exists c\in(a,b)$: $f'(c)=0$.
MVT: $\exists c\in(a,b)$: $f'(c)=\dfrac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}$.
Proof of inequality: MVT on $[0,x]$ or $[x,y]$, then bound $f'(c)$ using $a<c<b$.
Fails: corner $|x-a|$, cusp $x^{2/3}$, jump, $c$ at an endpoint.

5.2 Antiderivatives

$\int x^n=\frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}$ $(n\neq-1)$, $\int\frac{dx}x=\ln|x|$, $\int e^{kx}=\frac1ke^{kx}$, $\int b^x=\frac{b^x}{\ln b}$
$\int\sin=-\cos$, $\int\cos=\sin$, $\int\sec^2=\tan$, $\int\csc^2=-\cot$
$\int\sec x\tan x=\sec x$, $\int\csc x\cot x=-\csc x$
$\int\frac{dx}{1+x^2}=\arctan x$, $\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}=\arcsin x$, $\int\frac{dx}{a^2+x^2}=\frac1a\arctan\frac xa$
$\int\tan x=\ln|\sec x|$, $\int\sec x=\ln|\sec x+\tan x|$  (+C)
IVP: integrate, then use $y(x_0)=y_0$ to find $C$.

5.3 / 5.9 Substitution

$\int f(g(x))g'(x)\,dx=\int f(u)\,du$, $u=g(x)$.
Definite: $\int_a^bf(g(x))g'(x)\,dx=\int_{g(a)}^{g(b)}f(u)\,du$ — change the limits, don't go back to $x$.
Leftover $x$: express with $u$ (e.g. $x^2=u-1$).
$\int_0^r\sqrt{r^2-u^2}\,du=\frac{\pi r^2}4$, $\int_{-r}^r\sqrt{r^2-u^2}\,du=\frac{\pi r^2}2$.

5.4 Sums & area

$\Delta x=\frac{b-a}n$, right: $x_k=a+k\Delta x$, left: $a+(k-1)\Delta x$, mid: $a+(k-\frac12)\Delta x$.
$A=\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{k=1}^nf(x_k)\Delta x$.
$\sum1=n$, $\sum k=\frac{n(n+1)}2$, $\sum k^2=\frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}6$, $\sum k^3=\left[\frac{n(n+1)}2\right]^2$.

5.5 Definite integral

$\int_a^bf=\lim_{\max\Delta x_k\to0}\sum f(x_k^*)\Delta x_k$ = net signed area.
$\int_a^a=0$, $\int_b^a=-\int_a^b$, $\int_a^b=\int_a^c+\int_c^b$, $\int_a^bc\,dx=c(b-a)$.
Odd: $\int_{-a}^af=0$; even: $2\int_0^af$. $|g|$ / piecewise: split at break points.

5.6 FTC

$\int_a^bf(x)\,dx=F(b)-F(a)$ ($F'=f$, $f$ continuous).
$\frac{d}{dx}\int_a^xf(t)\,dt=f(x)$;  $\frac{d}{dx}\int_{h(x)}^{g(x)}f(t)\,dt=f(g)g'-f(h)h'$.

5.7 Motion via integrals

$v=\int a\,dt$, $s=\int v\,dt$ (two constants!).
Displacement $=\int_{t_0}^{t_1}v\,dt$; distance $=\int_{t_0}^{t_1}|v|\,dt$ (split where $v=0$).
Constant $a$: $v=v_0+at$, $s=s_0+v_0t+\frac12at^2$.

5.8 Average value

$f_{\text{ave}}=\dfrac1{b-a}\int_a^bf(x)\,dx$. MVT for integrals: $\exists c$: $f(c)=f_{\text{ave}}$.

5.10 ln as an integral

$\ln x=\int_1^x\frac{dt}t$ $(x>0)$, $\frac{d}{dx}\ln x=\frac1x$, $\ln1=0$, $\int_1^e\frac{dt}t=1$.
$F(x)=\int_a^xf(t)dt$: $F(a)=0$, $F'=f$ → analyse with §4.1.

7.2 Integration by parts

$\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du$;  $\int_a^bu\,dv=[uv]_a^b-\int_a^bv\,du$.
$u$ by LIATE: Log, Inverse trig, Algebraic, Trig, Exponential.
Polynomial × $e^{ax}$/$\sin$/$\cos$: tabular (signs $+,-,+,\dots$).
$\ln x$, $\arctan x$: $u=$ it, $dv=dx$. Cyclic ($e^x\sin x$, $\cos\ln x$): twice, solve for $I$.

7.3 Trig integrals

$\sin^m\cos^n$: $n$ odd → save $\cos$, $u=\sin$; $m$ odd → save $\sin$, $u=\cos$; both even → $\sin^2=\frac{1-\cos2x}2$, $\cos^2=\frac{1+\cos2x}2$.
$\tan^m\sec^n$: $n$ even → save $\sec^2$, $u=\tan$; $m$ odd → save $\sec\tan$, $u=\sec$; $\tan^2=\sec^2-1$.
$\sin A\cos B=\frac12[\sin(A-B)+\sin(A+B)]$
$\sin A\sin B=\frac12[\cos(A-B)-\cos(A+B)]$
$\cos A\cos B=\frac12[\cos(A-B)+\cos(A+B)]$
$\sin2x=2\sin x\cos x$, $\ 1+\tan^2=\sec^2$.

Derivatives you need

$(\tan)'=\sec^2$, $(\sec)'=\sec\tan$, $(\cot)'=-\csc^2$, $(\csc)'=-\csc\cot$, $(\arctan x)'=\frac1{1+x^2}$, $(\arcsin x)'=\frac1{\sqrt{1-x^2}}$, $(\ln|x|)'=\frac1x$, $(e^{g})'=g'e^g$.
